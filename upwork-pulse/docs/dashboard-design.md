# Dashboard design

The spec `skills/upwork-pulse/assets/dashboard.html` is built and reviewed against — for whoever changes the page. The skill does not read it at run time: it only publishes and updates the page (`skills/upwork-pulse/references/dashboard.md`).

The page is the user's live view of the pipeline. It reads Notion and Upwork with the viewer's own connectors, asks Claude (`sample`) for chat summaries, and computes everything else in the browser; Notion and Upwork stay the source of truth, and the chat with Claude stays the place for anything beyond the few clicks listed under **Rows**. It is for the user alone.

**Where state lives.** What the page derives and must keep is a chat's state — Claude's summary of its last message and whether the user parked it — and it lives in the artifact's `db` (collection `threads`, one document per Upwork room: `summary {v, storyId, data, at}`, `park {storyId, on, at}`), so a phone and a laptop show the same and a summary is made once. A view without `db` falls back to the browser's storage. The browser keeps only per-device conveniences (the drawer's width, a tab, folded sections) and caches it can rebuild (proposals known to be over, a proposal's chat, how a job ended, a chat's organisation — pruned as they leave the lists — and the hour-long pause on job pages after Upwork refuses one). A view without `db` says in Your move that summaries and parks stay in that browser. Notion is not used for this: a park belongs to an Upwork chat, which may have no card, and the page writes Notion only on a click.

## Layout

1180px wide, one screen on a laptop; phones stack the sections.

- **Header**: name, three pills — `replies` · `overdue` · `invitations` — always shown, coloured only when not zero, a click scrolls to Your move; `Live · updated hh:mm` — also the **status**: its dot is green while everything runs, red with a short note (`· job pages paused`, `· 3 issues`) when something is degraded, and a click lists each part with what is wrong — the job scanner (idle over 3 hours, or its last run could not read Search rules), job pages (paused by Upwork: Run state's `JOB_PAGES_PAUSE` and `DEFERRED` lines, `references/assess.md` §2), the Notion and Upwork connectors, a pipeline update pending or stopped, an outdated dashboard, a Proposal guide not filled while auto proposals are on —, Refresh, **Settings** (a popover with the `auto_drafts` / `auto_skip` switches, written to the config, the `chat_project` the job chats open in, shown read-only — it is changed in chat — and **Versions**: this page's, the config's `schema` and the `skill_version` the hourly run last used). A page older than the skill or the schema is one of the status's issues, and the banner over the leads.
- **Leads** (left): `New` cards from `Inbox` minus jobs already applied to, Take / Maybe / Skipped tabs (Skipped: the last 2 days, more on request, Auto / Manual on the right), an "Only ready" switch, a mark on the right of a row — "Proposal ready" / "Writing…" from the job's row in Proposals `Open`, "Not written" when it stays Writing past 30 minutes (the drafts task takes a request for that long, `references/drafts.md` §1), "Skip advised" — grouped by day, posting time on the right, the client's numbers against the user's estimate in a Client / Me grid, flags on each side (the client's on the Client line, a `duplicate` chip for a repeated title, connects "over balance" in amber); a green mark for a score at or above `match_score`. Jobs posted in the last 5 days are listed; older ones fold into "Older" at the bottom, and past 7 days a title is dimmed. Every value is a column shown as stored; the one text the page reads into numbers is the `Estimate` column, to check its lines add up to `My hours`.
- **Connects** and **Balance** tiles (right, top).
- **Your move**: everything that waits on the user, each thing once — contracts that need a reply or have an overdue milestone (a short row: what it needs — the overdue milestone named — and the summary; the card itself stays under Contracts), other chats where the next message is the user's or that the user has not read (a chat other than a contract's for 14 days after its last message; Upwork says whose turn it is for a proposal's or contract's chat; for a group chat or a direct message it does not, so the thread summary's `next` decides — a message from another freelancer on the team, or files and news shared for information, is not the user's move; until the summary is made, a last message from someone else counts as the user's turn; a chat with a message the user has not read is in Your move too, whatever its summary says), invitations and offers. Each chat and contract carries Claude's summary of the thread and the next step (`Your move: …`), made with `sample` from the last 12 messages and redone when a new message arrives; a chat's or contract's drawer opens with the same summary, the thread folded below it. An invitation shows its job card's advice (the hourly run gives every pending invitation a card) and opens that card's lead drawer — Write a proposal, Skip, Claude chat; until the card exists it says the next hourly run assesses it. An offer comes from a proposal: its row carries the terms (rate, duration) and that proposal's chat — unread count, summary — so the chat is not a second row, and it opens the proposal's drawer with the offer on top and **Answer the offer** in the bar, beside Claude chat and Reply on Upwork; the proposal's own drawer shows the same offer. **Parked**: a chat about getting work (a proposal, a direct message, an ended contract's chat — never an active contract's, nor a group chat or direct message put under one) that the client closed or put off — hired someone else, paused the project, will come back later — folds into `Parked · N` at the bottom of Your move, open or shut as the user left it, and counts in neither the section's number nor the pills. The thread summary decides it (`parked`, `parked_why`, `back_on`), or the user does with *Park until they write* / *Keep in Your move* on the chat's summary plaque, kept with the chat in `db` (*Where state lives*); either holds until the next message, and `back_on` brings the chat back on that day, in the user's timezone. Without summaries in a view (no `sample`), Your move says that chats are not parked by themselves there. A parked chat's row reads like any other, with no red bar and the thread's summary, and its chip elsewhere says Parked.
- **Footer**: the job scanner's last run (when, what it found, the models; "idle" after 3 hours) and the open questions for the weekly review, which open into a list with links to Notion.
- **Contracts**: every active contract, always — summary, milestone strip, the current milestone; one that needs the user has a red bar and is in Your move too.
- **Proposals**: Sent / In talks / Closed, live from Upwork, each tab showing what moved in the last 14 days; Closed also takes a proposal the client moved on from: Upwork puts it on hold (annotated OnHold) and leaves it out of its proposal lists, so a proposal Notion has as sent that no list returns is read by its id, and on hold means Closed; what is known for good (a closed proposal, a proposal's chat, how its job ended) is remembered in the browser, so a later load reads only the lists, and shows the ones with a chat first — After talks, how each ended and who wrote last — with No reply folded below; In talks lists every proposal chat, newest message first, each marked whose turn it is (the user's-turn ones are in Your move too). A proposal's drawer shows its job (the Jobs card: Client vs me, About the job) and **What you sent** — the row in Proposals `Sent`, or, until the next digest syncs it, the proposal as Upwork returns it (same blocks, no milestones, marked as from Upwork) — with links to both in Notion.
- **Rows**: the title opens the item on Upwork (the job, the thread for a reply, the workroom, the invitation); the rest of the row opens the **drawer** from the right — the lead's advice line, Client vs me, About the job and then the proposal from Proposals with a copy icon on every value, with Applied / Skip (a reason if you want one) / ✎ Write or ↻ Rewrite / Restore in a sticky bar, a contract's summary, milestones and conversation, a proposal's chat and terms — with the main Upwork action as a button, foldable sections, ↑ / ↓ through the list, and a widen button that makes the drawer as wide as the page (1180px, never wider than the window; remembered). Every drawer lists its sections in one order — Client vs me (first and always open, when the job has a card), About the job, the proposal or What you sent, Milestones, Conversation, Facts — and starts folded what matters less for its kind: About the job once the user has applied (an applied lead, a proposal, a contract, a conversation), What you sent in a proposal that is being discussed, the Conversation under its summary, Facts always. A New lead has Lock / Unlock in the bar, and a locked one shows `Locked` on its row and in the drawer's header; Write and Rewrite tick `Locked` on the card before starting the task (SKILL.md → *Locked*). Applied / Skip / Restore write `Status`, `Decided on` and the skip fields to the card, Lock / Unlock writes `Locked`, the switches write the config, Write / Rewrite sets the job's proposal row to `State` Writing (creating the row when there is none), ticks `Locked` and starts the drafts task; nothing else writes. The Skip form offers Claude's Skip advice as the reason, which the user can change or clear.
- **Claude chat**, in the drawer's top bar for a lead, a proposal, a contract or a conversation: the job's one Cowork chat. The link is the card's `Chat` — on a phone `https://claude.ai/cowork/cse_<id>`, elsewhere `claude://claude.ai/code/session_<id>` (artifacts let `claude://` out in the desktop app; iOS keeps claude.ai links in the browser). No `Chat` yet → a new Cowork whose first message names the job first and then its ids and the skill — `Job: <title> — Upwork Pulse, job #… (Job ID …)[, contract …]. Chat title: "Job: <title>". Load it with the upwork-pulse skill.`, for a conversation with no job `Client: <client> — Upwork Pulse, Upwork room <id>. Chat title: "Client: <client>". Load it with the upwork-pulse skill.` (the chat names itself where it can, since the app's own title is a guess) (`references/chat.md` → *A job's chat*); the link is `claude://cowork/new?q=…`, on a phone `https://claude.ai/new?mode=cowork&surface=cowork&project=<id>&q=…` with the project from `chat_project` (none → no `project`) — and that chat writes its own link to the card (`references/chat.md` → *A job's chat*). A contract finds its job through `list_contracts` `get` (`job.id`), its conversation through the contract; a conversation with no proposal or contract gets a chat that is not kept. When the job has its chat, a ▾ beside the button offers **New chat** (for when the old one is deleted or too long): the same first message with `Its old chat is gone: this is the job's chat from now on.` after the ids, so the new chat takes the card's `Chat` over.
- **↻ in the drawer's top bar**, for any drawer — a lead, a proposal, a contract, a conversation: reads everything again, like the header's Refresh, while the drawer stays open and repaints (its conversation read anew); its arrow turns until the loads end, as the header's Refresh (↻ and the word, like Settings) does. On a phone the bar keeps its buttons on one line, without the drawer's title.
- Same look as the project-tracker dashboard (tokens, pills, rows, drawer); change the shared parts in both together.
